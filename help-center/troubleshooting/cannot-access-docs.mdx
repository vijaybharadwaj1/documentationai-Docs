---
title: Cannot Access Documentation
description: What to do if you can't access Vijay Bharadwaj's documentation pages.
---

## Symptoms
- Pages not loading
- Error: "404 Not Found" or "Access Denied"

## Solutions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Login Status">
    Make sure you are logged in at `https://vijaybharadwaj.com/login`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Project Access">
    Confirm you have access to the specific documentation project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear Browser Cache">
    Try clearing your browser cache and reloading the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Support">
    If issues persist, email support@vijaybharadwaj.com.
  </Step>
</Steps>
