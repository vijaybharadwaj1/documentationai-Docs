## Overview

Follow these steps to organize your docs:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a New Project">
    Click "New Project" from your dashboard and add a project name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Folders">
    Use the "Add Folder" option to group related documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange Documents">
    Drag and drop docs to reorder within folders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Collaborators">
    Go to settings, click "Invite" and enter team members' emails.
  </Step>
</Steps>
